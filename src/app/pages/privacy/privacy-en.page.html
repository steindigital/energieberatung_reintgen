5. Storing your display setting (light/dark)

If you switch between the light and dark theme using the switch in the header, your choice is stored in your browser's local storage (localStorage) under the key theme-preference so that it is kept for your next visit. This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to me or to third parties. Storage is necessary for the function you have explicitly requested (Section 25 (2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete the entry at any time via your browser settings.
